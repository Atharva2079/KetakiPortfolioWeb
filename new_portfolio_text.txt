=== PAGE 5 ===
Why SLIQUE toaster
Rushed mornings create a need for low effort meals hence breakfast routines change. Some users need only a 
single slice, while others prepare several at once. This created an opportunity to rethink the toaster around 
flexibility, cleanliness and individual control in shared living and office environments .
Users POV
˝I don't like getting my hands buttery or sticky, especially when 
I'm in a rush. I'd rather skip eating than deal with the mess.˛ 
(Sourabh, 42)
˝I often use a toaster when I don't have time to prepare a full 
meal. It makes breakfast much quicker.˛ (Supriya, 29)

organization issues and chaos within the truck while managing the EV rescue operations.
