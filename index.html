SYNCFORCE

Multi-Functional Firefighter Rescue Tool

4 Months Individual Project Product Design Studio

The Challenge

Firefighters face high-pressure rescue scenarios with tools that are singular in function, leading to equipment overload and chaos within rescue trucks. SyncForce addresses this with a modular, multi-functional approach.

Research & Insights

Extensive field research with firefighters revealed the need for faster operation, reduced fatigue, effective emergency response, and modular tool design that can adapt to different rescue scenarios.

Combination Tool Design

A combination cutting-extinguisher tool with Storz couplings, replaceable nozzles (abrasive & foam), support handles, and individual water/abrasive inlet pipes. Built with stainless steel, naval brass, and tungsten carbide.
