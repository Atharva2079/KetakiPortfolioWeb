SLIQUE

A Multi-Slot Toaster for Varied Morning Routines

4 Months Studio Project Individual Design 5 Toasting Slots 1:1 Steel Prototype

Why SLIQUE Toaster?

Rushed mornings create an urgent need for low-effort, high-speed meals. However, traditional toasters force compromises: some family members only want a single slice, while others need four slices simultaneously with different browning preferences.

This challenge led Ketaki to rethink toaster architecture around individual control, cleanliness, and spatial efficiency in both shared living and busy office environments.

⚡ Simultaneous Multi-Toasting Without Waiting
🎚️ Independent Browning Across Slots
✨ Mess-Free Butter & Jam Prep
🧹 Smooth-Slide Crumb Handling

SYNCFORCE

A Modular Rescue Tool Engineered for EV Fire Emergencies

4 Months Studio Project 300 Bar Water Pressure Interchangeable Nozzles Field Research: 6 Fire Stations

The High-Pressure Reality of EV Fires

Electric vehicle battery thermal runaways burn at upwards of 1,000°C and cannot be extinguished with standard water hoses alone. Firetrucks carry specialized tools, but each tool serves an isolated, singular role—leading to equipment overload, disorganization, and dangerous delays at the accident scene.

On-Site Observational Fieldwork

Visit to 6 Fire Stations Across Pune

Ketaki conducted extensive observational research across 6 fire stations including the Pune Fire Brigade Headquarters, interviewing front-line first responders to study tool retrieval friction and fatigue under life-critical time spans.

Benchmark Studied: C 360 H Kit by Coldcut Cobra cutting-extinguisher system for heavy vehicle installation.
Operating Hydraulic Pressure HIGH PRESSURE READY
300 BAR

Delivers 300 bar piercing stream to puncture reinforced lithium battery enclosures safely from exterior standoff.

Select Active Rescue Mode:

Water + Tungsten Abrasive Mixture

Ultra-high pressure abrasive slurry punctures ultra-high-strength steel car bodywork and battery casings within seconds without generating thermal sparks.

AVOSPIN

Avocado-Shaped Sensory Planetary Gear Fidget Accessory

2 Months Elective 5-Day Rapid Build Sprint 6 Gear Iterations Functional Planetary Train

Dealing with Mental Health as a Teen

Sensory fidget tools are proven to calm hyperactive nervous systems, improve attention span, and ground teenagers during overwhelming anxiety or mundane classrooms. However, most fidget toys feel cheap, noisy, or childish.

AvoSpin transforms the fidget toy into a sophisticated lifestyle accessory. Inspired by the naturally curvy, textured teardrop silhouette of an avocado—a cultural icon deeply embedded in Gen-Z aesthetics—it conceals an intricate functional planetary gear train within an ergonomic palm-sized form.

1:1 Palm Ergonomics
5 Days Build & Rebuild Sprint
6x Gear Builds Iterated

Rethinking Compression Therapy

Traditional static compression bandages and stockings suffer from massive patient non-compliance: they are excruciatingly painful to put on (donning) and remove (doffing), slip out of calibrated pressure gradients, and offer zero feedback on therapeutic efficacy.

Ketaki led the comprehensive design study for Acticompress, developing static product physical ergonomics, brand identity, visual communication, DFMA for wearable sleeves, and the companion cross-platform mobile and clinical web dashboards.
